Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 26, 2026

HOA Music Player Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

By using the app, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy. If you do not agree with these terms, please do not use the application.

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

HOA Music Player Pro is an Offline Local Music Player. Our primary philosophy is that your music and your data belong to you. We do not require an account, we do not track your listening habits on our servers, and we do not sell your personal data to third parties.

2. Information We Collect

A. Personal Data

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

B. Media Files

The app accesses your local audio files to provide music playback. This metadata (song titles, artist names, album art) is processed locally on your device to build your library and is never transmitted to our servers.

C. Technical Information

Standard technical information required for the app to function (such as device model, OS version, and language settings) is used locally to optimize the interface and audio engine performance.

3. Permissions & Usage

To provide a professional audio experience, the app requires the following permissions:

  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_AUDIO: Required to scan, index, and play the music files stored on your device.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Required for features like the Tag Editor and downloading album art (if enabled).
  • BLUETOOTH_CONNECT: Required to manage playback through Bluetooth headphones and car audio systems.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required to show playback controls in your device notification tray.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Prevents the audio engine from being killed by the system when the screen is off.

4. Third-Party Services

We use a limited number of trusted third-party components to enhance your experience:

  • Media3 / ExoPlayer: Used for the core audio playback engine.
  • Last.fm (Optional): If you manually enable Scrobbling, your listening data is sent to Last.fm according to their privacy policy.
  • Google Play Billing: Handles secure transactions for the Pro version. We do not see or store your credit card information.

5. Data Security

Since your data is stored locally, its security depends on the security of your device. We recommend keeping your Android OS updated and using device encryption.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

7. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

For users in the European Economic Area (GDPR) or California (CCPA): Because we do not collect personal data on our servers, your "Right to Access" and "Right to be Forgotten" are fulfilled by your ability to manage or delete the app and its local data directly on your device.
